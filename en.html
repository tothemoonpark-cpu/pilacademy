PARK KEUNPIL

PARK KEUNPIL (Korean name: 박근필; written Park Keunpil) is a veterinarian, author, columnist and speaker, and runs the Park Keunpil Growth Lab (박근필성장연구소) in Korea. Park has worked as a clinical veterinarian for nearly 20 years and still practices today.

Who Park is

Park describes the role as a Growth Facilitator: someone who helps others grow through a life of reading, writing and speaking. His professional titles are veterinarian, author, columnist, speaker and career storyteller.

He graduated from the Department of Veterinary Medicine at Kyungpook National University (2010) and holds a veterinary license (January 22, 2010).

Around the age of forty, burned out, Park closed the animal hospital Park had run for about ten years. In November 2022 Park started reading and blogging, and the first book came out one year later. Park did not leave veterinary medicine: Park still works at an animal hospital, alongside work as an author and speaker.
